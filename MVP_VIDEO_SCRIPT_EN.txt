SPRINTOS AI REVIEWER - MVP VIDEO SCRIPT
English recording script for the Instawards submission
Date: 16 September 2026


MAIN VIDEO FLOW - READ THIS SCRIPT ON CAMERA

Target duration: 1:15-1:30 for the landing page, followed by approximately
2:30 in the app. Each section tells you what to show, what to click, what to
read, and what to say.

DEMO SETUP
- Repository: https://github.com/egekoca/SprintOS
- Milestone 1: Stellar milestone settlement contract - 500 USDC
- Milestone 2: AI assisted advisory review and security boundary - 300 USDC
- Milestone 3: Public SprintOS web MVP and verifiable evidence flow - 200 USDC
- Total testnet award: 1,000 USDC
- Project admin: the wallet that creates the project and writes the milestones;
  it is also the sponsor and the default human reviewer.
- Builder: a separate testnet wallet that submits evidence and receives payment.
- Additional admin/reviewer wallets are optional. The builder can never be one.

Before recording, confirm that the testnet wallet, testnet USDC, Blob storage,
the advisory service and /api/health are ready. Never show a seed phrase,
private key, API key or storage token.


PART A - LANDING PAGE (TARGET: 1:15-1:30)

LANDING 1 - OPENING HERO (00:00-00:15 / 15 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Open https://sprintos-ai.vercel.app/.
2. Stay at the top of the page. Keep the SprintOS logo, “Built. Proven. Paid.”
   headline and “Open SprintOS” button in the frame.
3. Do not click during the first 15 seconds; let the hero animation finish.

Read this:
"Hello, I am Ege. Today I will show you SprintOS. SprintOS is an AI-assisted
milestone payment and review application running on the Stellar testnet. The
project admin defines the work and its acceptance criteria. The builder submits
public proof. AI prepares an advisory review, while the decision to approve or
move money always belongs to a human wallet."

Read on screen:
- Built. Proven. Paid.
- Fund a milestone. Prove the work. A person releases the payment.


LANDING 2 - THE PROBLEM (00:15-00:30 / 15 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Scroll down one full section to the #problem section.
2. Slowly move across the cards: “The target moves”, “The proof scatters”,
   and “The money waits”. Do not click the cards.

Read this:
"Grant, freelance and contributor payments usually have three problems. The
target can change during the project. Proof is scattered across GitHub, tests,
documentation and messages. Finally, payment depends on trust or a slow manual
review instead of a clear record. SprintOS brings all three parts into one flow."


LANDING 3 - THE SOLUTION (00:30-00:45 / 15 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Scroll down to the #solution section.
2. Follow the flow from left to right: “Scope is fixed” -> “Work is attached”
   -> “A person signs” -> “Money moves”.

Read this:
"SprintOS has four steps. First, the scope is fixed. Then the work is attached
to a GitHub repository. AI reads the evidence and prepares a report for the
reviewer. A named human wallet approves or holds the work, and signs a separate
transaction to release payment. AI speeds up review; it does not control the
decision or the money."


LANDING 4 - ESCROW AND MILESTONES (00:45-01:00 / 15 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Scroll to the #escrow section.
2. Show “One budget, three proofs” and the escrow tree leading to three
   milestones.
3. Keep the illustration visible for a moment. Those illustration amounts are
   not the demo amounts; the app demo will use 500, 300 and 200 USDC.

Read this:
"One budget is divided into separate deliverables. Each milestone has a title,
acceptance criteria, a deadline and an amount. The illustration shows the idea;
in the application I will create a real SprintOS plan with 500, 300 and 200
testnet USDC."


LANDING 5 - LIVE TOTALS (01:00-01:12 / 12 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Scroll to the Live Totals section.
2. Wait for the totals to load and keep the cards visible.

Read this:
"These totals are not an application estimate. They summarize funded and
released milestones read from the Stellar testnet ledger. Funding an engagement
does not mean the builder has been paid. Payment appears only after the human
release signature."


LANDING 6 - TRUST BOUNDARY AND ENTERING THE APP (01:12-01:30 / 18 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Scroll to the #trust section.
2. Show the two columns: “The software / Reads and reports” and “A named person
   / Reads and decides”.
3. Read the lines “No wallet. No signing key.” and “The payment moves because
   they signed.”
4. Scroll to the bottom and click “Enter SprintOS”.

Read this:
"The trust boundary is explicit. The software reads public evidence, compares
it with the criteria and returns a score. It has no wallet, signing key or
payment method. The named human reads the same record and decides with their
own wallet. I will now show the same flow inside the application."


PART B - APPLICATION (TARGET: ABOUT 2:30)

Read this:

am connecting the project admin wallet. This wallet creates and funds the

does not hold the private key; it hands signing requests to the wallet. I am
opening a new project now."


APP 2 - ATTACH THE REPOSITORY (01:42-01:57 / 15 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. On /projects/new, keep Step 01, “Repository”, visible.
2. Select the “Public repository” mode.
3. Enter: https://github.com/egekoca/SprintOS
4. Click “Scan repository”.
5. Show the SprintOS name, default branch and any milestone or open-item count.
6. Leave “Attached to this engagement” visible and click “Continue to scope”.
   If GitHub milestones are returned, select up to three and click “Use ...
   milestones”. Otherwise continue with a Manual plan.

Read this:
"I am attaching the official SprintOS repository to this engagement. Because
the repository is public, I can scan it by URL without GitHub OAuth. This link
defines where the builder's public proof will come from. The repository is
attached, so I am moving on to the delivery plan."


APP 3 - CREATE THE THREE MILESTONES (01:57-02:27 / 30 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. On Step 02, select “Manual”.
2. Create three milestone cards and enter these values.

MILESTONE 1
Title: Stellar milestone settlement contract
Outcome: The Stellar testnet flow works from engagement creation and USDC
funding through evidence submission, human approval, separate release and
deadline refund.
   Acceptance criteria:
   1. The contract creates and funds a testnet-USDC engagement with separate
      project-admin and builder roles and no more than three milestones.
   2. After builder evidence, a human reviewer approves and a separate release
      signature transfers the milestone amount to the builder.
Amount: 500 USDC

MILESTONE 2
Title: AI assisted advisory review and security boundary
Outcome: A public evidence review report is generated, while the security
boundary proves that AI cannot move money.
   Acceptance criteria:
   1. The advisory report returns a score, criterion checklist, missing
      information, recommendation, binding:false and a report_hash.
   2. The advisory package has no wallet or signing/payment path; milestone
      state and USDC movement change only after a human wallet signature.
Amount: 300 USDC

MILESTONE 3
Title: Public SprintOS web MVP and verifiable evidence flow
Outcome: The public web MVP connects the project admin, builder and reviewer
flows with repository evidence, wallet signatures, hashes and explorer links.
   Acceptance criteria:
   1. The project admin can attach a public repository, define milestones, assign
      the builder and fund the engagement in /projects/new.
   2. The builder submits hashed public evidence; the reviewer reads the criteria
      and evidence, signs approve and release, and /e/:id shows the public log.
Amount: 200 USDC

3. The first milestone may use a historical start date, but every due date must
   still be in the future when you sign. Choose the first due date, add the next
   milestones with “+ Add milestone”, and show that each new start date is
   automatically one day after the previous due date. Keep all due dates in the
   future for the demo.
4. Enter `1000` in “Total award” and click “Split across 3 milestones”.
5. Show the readout: 500 / 300 / 200 USDC, total 1,000 USDC.
6. Click “Continue to roles”.

Read this:
"I am turning SprintOS into three checkable milestones. The first is the
settlement contract. The second is the advisory report and its security boundary.
The third is the public web MVP that connects both flows. Each milestone has an
outcome, acceptance criteria, a deadline and a value. I am allocating the
1,000-testnet-USDC demo as 500, 300 and 200."

APP 5 - FINAL REVIEW, CREATE AND FUND (02:42-03:00 / 18 seconds)

Screen and actions:

   admin, milestone dates, criteria and amounts.
2. Check “I have read every milestone and requirement above”.
3. Click “Continue to sign”, then “Yes, lock and sign”.
4. Confirm the create transaction in the wallet.
5. Show “Engagement created”, the engagement ID and the create transaction hash.

Read this:
"The final receipt shows exactly what the project admin is committing: the
repository, builder, admin authority, dates, criteria and amounts. The checkbox
is a real read-through attestation. The create signature hashes and fixes the
milestone terms on the ledger. The second signature puts 1,000 testnet USDC in
escrow. The builder has not been paid yet; the money is locked to the defined work."

APP 7 - REVIEWER DESK AND AI SCORE (03:20-03:45 / 25 seconds)

Screen and actions:
1. Switch the wallet back to the project admin wallet, which is the default
   human reviewer for this engagement.
2. Open /review.
3. Under “Waiting on you”, open the new EvidenceSubmitted milestone. The URL is
   /review/{NEW_ID}/0.
4. Show “matches chain” on Acceptance criteria and Submitted evidence.
5. Click “Generate report” in the advisory panel.
6. Read the score, checklist, recommendation, missing information, binding:false
   and report hash.

Read this:
"The reviewer desk puts three things together: what the project admin agreed to,
what the builder submitted, and what the advisory module thinks. Both documents
match the hashes recorded on chain. I am requesting the report now. AI returns a
score, criterion checklist, missing information and a recommendation. The report
is explicitly binding:false. It is not an approval, it is not a payment command,
and it is never passed to the settlement contract as an argument."
